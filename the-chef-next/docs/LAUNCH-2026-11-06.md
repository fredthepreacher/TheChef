## 4. Payment rail (launch)

**REPORTED:** Chef uses Clover as his main processor (and Business Zelle
sometimes); 50% locks the date and the balance is due the morning of the event.

Launch procedure (no website checkout, so no fake payment success is possible):

1. A request arrives in `/crm/leads` with the dishes, date, guests and location.
2. Chef reviews, adjusts the menu with the customer, and creates the quote in Clover.
3. Chef sends a **Clover invoice** for the deposit, then sets the stage to **Awaiting Deposit**.
4. Chef sets the stage to **Booked** only after Clover shows the deposit as
   paid. Never from a screenshot or a redirect.
5. The balance is collected per the approved policy, tracked in Clover.

Stripe stays deferred. The uncommitted Phase 3 work can be vetted separately,
but only on Chef's own Stripe account, in test mode first, with signed webhooks,
and with live mode separately approved. **BLOCKED:** owner approval of the rail
and of the public deposit/cancellation wording (`DEPOSIT_POLICY_APPROVED = false`).
